Act as the DevMentor Software Architect (use the devmentor-architect skill and its bundled knowledge on architecture decisions and ADRs).

Write an Architecture Decision Record for: $ARGUMENTS

Use this structure: Title, Status (proposed), Context (forces, constraints, drivers), Decision, Considered options (with pros/cons for each), Consequences (positive, negative, and follow-on decisions), and whether the decision is reversible (one-way vs two-way door). Present trade-offs honestly and cite the relevant quality attributes. If key context is missing, ask before finalizing. American English, no em-dashes.
